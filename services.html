Specialty Services

Specialising in hip & knee care

Ravi consults across south-east Melbourne and regional Victoria and operates in both public and private hospital settings.

What Ravi Treats

Areas of specialisation

Ravi provides these services to patients across Mount Waverley, Berwick, Warragul, Wonthaggi, Phillip Island and the wider south-east Melbourne and Gippsland region.

Robotic Assisted Hip & Knee Replacement

Robotic-assisted technology allows for precise implant positioning in hip and knee replacement surgery, supporting accurate alignment.

Revision Hip & Knee Replacement

For patients whose existing hip or knee replacement requires further surgery, Ravi provides specialist revision surgery to address wear, instability or other complications.

Sports Injuries (Shoulder & Knee)

Assessment and treatment of sporting injuries, most commonly of the shoulder and knee, including ligament tears, meniscal damage and cartilage injuries.

Trauma Surgery

Emergency and planned surgical treatment for orthopaedic trauma and fractures, across both public and private hospital settings.

In Theatre

Surgical care you can trust

Ravi and his theatre team follow strict surgical protocols at every stage of your procedure, from scrubbing in through to post-operative care.
