Orthopaedic Surgeon

Dr Ravi Dissanayake

Dr Ravi Dissanayake is an Australian fellowship-trained orthopaedic surgeon, specialising in revision hip and knee replacement, robotic assisted hip and knee replacement, sports knee injuries, and trauma surgery. Ravi consults across south-east Melbourne and regional Victoria and operates in both public and private hospital settings.

Specialising In

Hip & knee replacement

Ravi's primary focus is hip and knee replacement, including complex revision surgery, alongside sports knee injuries and trauma care. He consults across south-east Melbourne and regional Victoria — if you're looking for an orthopaedic surgeon near Mount Waverley, Berwick, Warragul, Wonthaggi or Phillip Island, Ravi holds regular clinics in each area.

Revision Hip & Knee Replacement

Surgery for patients whose existing hip or knee replacement requires revision — a core focus of Ravi's practice.

Robotic Assisted Hip & Knee Replacement

Joint replacement surgery using robotic-assisted technology for precise implant positioning.

Sports Knee Injuries

Assessment and treatment of sporting knee injuries, from ligament tears to cartilage damage.

Trauma Surgery

Emergency and planned surgical care for orthopaedic trauma and fractures.
